# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Open in BigText from the Explorer, an editor tab and the editor (at the caret's line).
- Open Folder in BigText.
- Offer BigText for files larger than `bigtext.suggestAboveMB` megabytes (50 by default).
- Finds BigText where its installers and package managers put it, or uses `bigtext.path`.
- Texts in English, Russian, Chinese, Spanish, Portuguese, German and Turkish.
